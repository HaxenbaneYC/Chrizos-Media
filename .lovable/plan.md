# Strengthen the favicon, page rhythm, and contact options

## What will change

- Replace the tiny full-logo browser tab image with a large, tightly framed lightning bolt taken from the approved Chrizos Media logo. Export it at favicon size with transparent padding so it remains recognizable in Chrome, Edge, and Safari.
- Remove stale internal placeholder wording from the Instagram accessibility label, the obsolete calendar illustration, and email preview data. Keep normal form hints such as “Your name” because those are purposeful visitor guidance, not unfinished copy.
- Give the long page visual rhythm while preserving the brand palette:
  - electric blue for the hero, services, and booking moments
  - deep navy for the Problem, proof, and About sections
  - retain existing glass panels and white typography for continuity
- Keep the trust layer factual. Preserve the current honest first-five-clients positioning and the single verified break-even result; do not add credentials or certifications that were not supplied.
- Keep “Contact” in the navigation and make its destination clearly acknowledge all three contact paths: Calendly, WhatsApp, and the enquiry form.
- Add a prominent WhatsApp contact button beside the booking experience using **+971 50 425 4366**, opening a prefilled chat in a new tab. Keep Calendly as the primary booking path and the enquiry form as the secondary written option.
- Replace the free-text-only service choice with a quick-select field for Paid Advertising, Content Strategy, Brand Strategy & Market Insights, or General Enquiry, while retaining a separate message box for context.

## Technical details

- Derive the favicon from the existing approved lightning artwork rather than generating or recoloring a new symbol.
- Add semantic section-surface tokens in the global design system and apply them to the existing sections without changing their order.
- Update the contact submission and email template to carry the selected service alongside the visitor’s message.
- Verify favicon output, accessibility labels, section contrast, WhatsApp destination, form submission fields, Calendly rendering, and mobile/desktop layout.
